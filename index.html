Privacy Policy for Arrow Puzzle

App Name: Arrow Puzzle  |  Package ID: com.aistudio.arrowpuzzle.kzrqpt
Effective Date: November 14, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Puzzle handles information when you use our Android mobile game. We respect your privacy and aim to collect and process only the minimum information required to operate the game and display advertisements.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Puzzle does not require user accounts, login credentials, or registration. The developer does not directly collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, physical address, or contacts.

  • On-Device Game Storage: Your unlocked levels (2D and 3D modes), saved hints, theme preference (light/dark), and sound settings are stored locally on your device using Android WebView local storage. This data stays on your device.
  • Automatically Collected Advertising & Diagnostic Data: Third-party advertising services integrated into the app (Google AdMob) may automatically collect device and interaction information as described below.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

Arrow Puzzle uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, to earn extra hints or continue after running out of lives).

When ads are loaded or shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use data such as:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID) and other device/app instance identifiers
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate general/coarse location)
  • Device & technical details (device model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • Ad interactions & diagnostics (ad impressions, clicks, crash logs, and performance telemetry)

You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services and review Google's Privacy Policy at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Security Information

Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. External Content Delivery Network (CDN)

To render 3D cube puzzle graphics, the app loads the Three.js library over HTTPS from a public Content Delivery Network (CDN). Standard network request metadata (such as your IP address and browser user-agent) may be processed by the CDN provider strictly to deliver the file.

4. App Permissions

Arrow Puzzle requests only minimal permissions required for gameplay and advertisements:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To check internet availability, load Google AdMob ads, and load 3D graphics scripts.
  • VIBRATE: To provide optional tactile vibration feedback during puzzle interactions.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, precise GPS location, or external media files.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Puzzle is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by applicable local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party services, please contact us so we can assist.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your game progress and preferences are saved locally on your device, you can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling Arrow Puzzle. Data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's data retention policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal/policy requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
